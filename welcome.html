Night Neutralizer

For watching in a dark room without turning the screen up or the volume down. Dark scenes get opened up, glare gets pulled back, and quiet dialogue comes up to meet the loud parts — the peaks are left where they are.

The same scene, twice

A night interior, a hard cut to daylight, and a fade back. The right-hand side is the real tone curve — measured from these frames the way it is measured from a film's — so what it does here is what it does to a video.

Three things worth knowing

  • Click the moon in the toolbar on any page with a player. The line under the switch says what is happening on that tab, the meter under it says how much, and Hold to compare shows the site's own output for as long as you hold it.
  • Netflix, Prime Video and Disney+ get a fixed curve, because protected video cannot be measured. Brightness on protected video in More options sets how much it is dimmed.
  • Nothing leaves your browser. One permission, no network requests, no accounts. Settings travel only through Chrome's own sync.
